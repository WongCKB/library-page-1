A-Z Free Online Resources

AIP Open Access 2012

AIP Open Access 2012

This offers online access to a collection close to 1 million articles from leading physical sciences publications including journals, conference proceedings, standards, and magazines published by AIP Publishing and its publishing partners.

arXiv.org

arXiv.org

Open access to e-prints in Physics, Mathematics, Computer Science, Quantitative Biology, Quantitative Finance and Statistics. Some of these articles may not be peer reviewed. Guide from publisher: (https://arxiv.org/help)

CERN Document Server

CERN Document Server

Particle physics articles, preprints, books, proceedings papers, etc from CERN (European Organisation for Nuclear Research).

Digital Commons Network

Digital Commons Network

The Digital Commons Network brings together scholarship from hundreds of universities and colleges, providing open access to peer-reviewed journal articles, book chapters, dissertations, working papers, conference proceedings and other original scholarly work. This constantly growing body of publications is curated by university librarians and their supporting institutions, and represents thousands of disciplines and subject areas.

Directory of Open Access Journals

Directory of Open Access Journals

A free online directory that indexes and provides full-text access to quality open access, peer-reviewed scholarly journals.

Elsevier Open Access Journals

Elsevier Open Access Journals

Articles published in Elsevier open access journals are peer reviewed and are free to access and download from ScienceDirect.

Hindawi Publishing Open Access

Hindawi Publishing Open Access

Hindawi publishes 438 peer-reviewed open access journals covering all areas of Science, Technology, and Medicine, as well as several areas of Social Sciences.

INSPIRE-HEP

INSPIRE-HEP

High-energy Physics literature database maintained by CERN, DESY, Fermilab, and SLAC.

J-STAGE

J-STAGE

J-STAGE is an open access system which supports science and technology information-related electronic journal release in Japan. Powered by the Japan Link Center (JaLC), research papers released on J-STAGE are linked to papers on various worldwide scholarly information services via PubMed, CAS Full Text Options, NASA ADS, MathSciNet, and CrossRef. Cross search services are also available with search engines or services such as Google, SciVerse Scopus, ProQuest, etc.

LibreTexts Physics Library

LibreTexts Physics Library

Explore courses, textbooks, homework exercises and ancillary materials. Topics include: Optics TextMaps, Quantum Mechanics, and Relativity.

Lumen Learning - Physics

Lumen Learning - Physics

Lumen Learning provides a textbook on Physics with various chapters such as Two-Dimensional Kinematics, Magnetism, and Geometric Optics.

Merlot

Merlot

This repository is one of the biggest and more famous places to find and share teaching resources.

MIT Open Courseware - Physics

MIT Open Courseware - Physics

Massachusetts Institute of Technology is one of the forerunners in open courseware, making 'virtually all MIT course content' available online for public access.

Nature Open Access Journals

Nature Open Access Journals

Nature Publishing Group (NPG) publishes over 70 journals that are open access or offer open access options. In total, NPG published almost 5,000 open access articles.

OER Commons - Physics

OER Commons - Physics

A list of open educational resources in Physics.

Open Source Physics

Open Source Physics

The OSP Collection provides curriculum resources in physics, computation, and computer modeling.

Open Textbook Library - Physics

Open Textbook Library - Physics

Supported by the Centre for Open Education and the Open Textbook Network.

Physclips

Physclips

A multimedia introduction to areas of Physics. Currently, it has a fairly complete introduction to mechanics and has resources for electricity and magnetism.

Physics - OpenStax

Physics - OpenStax

Based on a two-semester introductory algebra-based physics course.

Physics Classroom

Physics Classroom

A collection of Physics tutorials that cover basic topics. Newton's laws, vectors, momentum, work and energy, rotational motion, optics, relativity, electricity and magnetism are all represented.

Public Library of Science (PLoS)

Public Library of Science (PLoS)

PLOS is a non-profit publisher that produces peer-reviewed journals in science and medicine. It advocates open access, applying the Creative Commons Attribution (CC BY) license (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) to their works.

The National Academies Press

The National Academies Press

A resource that provides textbooks on various topics on astronomy, astrophysics, and space exploration.

UCLA Physics & Astronomy Resource Lab

UCLA Physics & Astronomy Resource Lab

Physics lab resources include instructional video, demonstration and lab manuals.

Wiley-Blackwell Open Access Collection

Wiley-Blackwell Open Access Collection

Wiley Open Access publishes authoritative peer reviewed open access journals across many research disciplines, such as biological, chemical and health sciences. These open access journals are supported by a network of authoritative journals and societies as well as internationally renowned editorial board members. All research articles published in Wiley Open Access journals are immediately freely available to read, download and share.

Yale Physics I & Physics II Lectures

Yale Physics I & Physics II Lectures

These lectures cover numerous fields including atomic physics and quantum optics; nuclear physics; particle physics; astrophysics and cosmology; condensed matter; quantum information physics and applied physics.
